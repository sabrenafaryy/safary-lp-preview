05What we do

What we actually do about it.

We measure to the seated case, not the form fill.

Google optimises toward whatever you call a conversion. Tell it “form submitted” and it finds people who submit forms.

So we work with you to capture what happened at each consult — showed, and closed — and report against that. Ten minutes of your coordinator’s month, and it’s the only way the spend can be judged on cases instead of on forms.

Almost no competitor will do this, because it means agreeing how the data gets back to us rather than reporting the booking and stopping.

Your consults arrive already able to pay.

Financing pre-qualification happens before the appointment, so the patient has made peace with the number before they’re in the chair and your coordinator spends the hour on treatment rather than sticker shock.

And we publish what “qualified” means — competitors sell the phrase and define it nowhere, so the definition itself is the proof.

And your advertising stays where your board expects it.

Dental advertising is regulated and the responsibility sits with the dentist, not the agency.
