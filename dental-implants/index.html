01The sentence we hear every week

“My consults show up. They don’t seat.”

Implant traffic is the most expensive in dentistry and it still works, because a single case is worth $12,000+ and a full arch is worth $20,000–$40,000.

What breaks is everything between the click and the treatment plan — and almost nobody measures that stretch.

02What we do

What we actually do about it.

01

We feed the seated case back to the platforms.

Consult-attended and case-accepted get imported as offline conversions, so Google starts hunting for patients who seat rather than patients who enquire. Your cost per lead usually goes up when this is working — and your cost per case goes down.

This is the whole offer, and it’s the thing almost no competitor will do, because it means touching the practice management system.

02

Full arch and single tooth stop sharing a budget.

A $40,000 full-arch patient and a $4,000 single-implant patient answer different ads, need different proof, and can bear completely different acquisition costs. Blended into one campaign, the cheap leads make the numbers look healthy while the expensive cases quietly go unbought.

We split them at campaign level, with their own pages and their own targets.

03

Your consults arrive already able to pay.

Financing pre-qualification happens before the appointment, so the patient has made peace with the number before they’re in the chair and your coordinator spends the hour on treatment rather than sticker shock.

And we publish what “financially qualified” means — competitors sell the phrase and define it nowhere, so the definition itself is the proof.

04

We work the consults you already paid for.

Only 30–40% of implant leads ever reach a consultation, and a share of those who attend never seat. That’s a named list of people you’ve already bought once.

It gets worked on a schedule, by email — not left sitting in the system.
