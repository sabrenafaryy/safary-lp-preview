07Questions

The things people always ask

How is this different from an agency?

Most agencies stop at the click — impressions, clicks, a monthly report. I don't stop until it's a booked appointment, and I tie every one to what you spent. You're buying booked revenue, not activity.

How do I know it'll beat what I'm already doing?

Because I'll prove it before you commit to anything ongoing. Send me your best-performing campaign and I'll build mine to run against it in your own account. Same budget — we split what you're already spending, so it costs you nothing extra in media. Same 30 days. We agree in writing what counts as a booked appointment before anything starts, and both campaigns report into the same dashboard, so neither of us is grading our own homework. If mine doesn't come in at least 20% cheaper per booked appointment, the $1,500 is refunded in full and you keep the tracking, the funnel and every lead it produced.

What does the test cost?

$1,500, once. That's the whole cost — no retainer, no contract, and no extra ad spend, because we split the budget you're already running rather than adding to it. If I don't beat your control by 20%, you get the $1,500 back.

Do I have to increase my ad budget for this?

No. That's deliberate. We split your existing spend — half stays on your campaign, half runs mine, for 30 days. Matching budgets instead of splitting them would mean asking you to double your media to find out whether I'm any good, and that's not a fair test to charge someone for.

Do I have to switch CRMs or change my systems to try this?

No. Nothing. During the test the follow-up runs on my system and the leads come to you — so the only thing I need from you is access to the ad account you're already running. You find out whether this works before you change a single thing about how you operate. If you decide to keep it, that's when I build it into your systems properly.

What if I can't measure my cost per booked appointment right now?

Most people can't, and that's the first thing we fix. Tracking gets installed before the test starts so both campaigns are measured the same way, by the same system, visible to you the whole time. Most people find their real number is nothing like what the ad platform reports. Either way, every lead the test produces is yours.

What happens after the test?

Entirely up to you — nothing renews automatically. If it worked and you want to keep it running, that's a monthly engagement, and the first thing it buys is your own version of the machine — the funnel, the tracking and the automation rebuilt inside your systems, in your name, with full export written into the agreement. $750 of what you paid for the test comes off your first month. If you'd rather stop, you stop. You keep every lead the test produced and your ad account is untouched.

What do I actually own?

Everything that matters, and it's worth being precise about when. Your ad accounts are yours the whole way through — in your name, funded by you, never marked up. Every lead the test produces is yours, delivered to you as it comes in, and yours to keep whether we continue or not. During the 30-day test, the follow-up runs on my system — my CRM, my automation, my tracking. That's deliberate: it means you don't have to install anything, migrate anything or change how you work to find out whether this beats what you've got. If we continue, I build that machine inside your own system and hand you the keys — tracking, funnel, automation, full data export, all written into the agreement. If we ever stop after that, nothing walks out the door with me.

Do you work in my industry?

The system is industry-agnostic — the mechanics of tracking, answering fast, qualifying, and booking work whether you're a clinic, a contractor, or a lender. On the call I'll tell you honestly whether it fits your numbers.
